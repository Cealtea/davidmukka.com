Notable Projects

Acting Craftsmanship

From emotionally charged performances to gripping character portrayals, I have etched my mark on the silver screen and small screens alike with a diverse range of roles. Each role is a canvas, and I try to paint all of them with authenticity and truth. Notable projects include: Black Ops: The Archives, Prespav (Season 6), The Elephant and Ingrid & I.

Harmony in Versatility

Beyond the silver screen, my musical journey is a symphony of genres. Music is the most. immediate form of art, and as a musician, I've written, composed. and performed albums, projects, original soundtracks and scores.

Whether it's composing soulful melodies, infusing film scenes with audible scopes or setting the stage ablaze, I strive to bring a unique rhythm and pitch to every endeavor.
